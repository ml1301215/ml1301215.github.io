    I enjoy reading and thinking. I'm currently reading Finite and Infinite Games《有限与无限的游戏》by James P. Carse. The book describes two kinds of games: a finite game is played to win — it has fixed rules, clear boundaries, and a final score. An infinite game is played to keep playing — the goal is to keep the game going, to keep experiencing. Perhaps AI is well-suited to finite games. But there is another kind of game, where the point is the playing itself.

    我喜欢阅读与思考。目前正在读詹姆斯·卡斯的《有限与无限的游戏》。书中描述了两种游戏：有限游戏为赢而玩——有固定规则、明确边界和最终比分；无限游戏为继续玩而玩——目标是让游戏延续下去，持续体验。AI或许擅长有限游戏，但还有另一种游戏，游戏本身才是意义所在。
