    Can a Lightweight AI Pipeline Solve Research-Level Math?

    February 2026  ·  5 min read

    We tested whether a small automated pipeline — LLM solve, Lean 4 verify, LLM correct — could make meaningful progress on problems that have no published solutions. The short answer: on competition-level problems, yes. On true research-level problems, not yet — but the failures are informative.

    Bogomolov on Quasi-Elliptic Surfaces

    October 2025  ·  6 min read

    The Bogomolov inequality bounds the second Chern class of a semistable vector bundle in terms of the first. In characteristic zero, the proof is classical. In characteristic 2 and 3, where quasi-elliptic fibrations appear, the standard arguments break down. Here I explain what goes wrong and how to fix it.

    Finite Games and Infinite Games

    June 2025  ·  3 min read

    James Carse distinguishes two kinds of games. A finite game is played to win; an infinite game is played to continue playing. Most AI benchmarks are finite games — there is a score, a ranking, a winner. Mathematics, I think, is an infinite game. The point is not to solve all problems but to keep the conversation going. I wonder what it would mean for AI to play the infinite game.
