About Me

Hi! I am a master's student in Mathematics at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), advised by Prof. Zhang Lei. My research spans algebraic geometry, AI for mathematics, and AI memory systems. I received my B.S. from the Gifted Young Programme at USTC in 2023.

I work on getting language models to produce and verify formal mathematical proofs, building agents with persistent memory across long conversations, and applying LLMs to security problems.
