    About me

    Hi! I am a master's student in Mathematics at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), advised by Prof. Zhang Lei.

    I work on getting language models to produce and verify formal mathematical proofs, building agents with persistent memory across long conversations, and applying LLMs to security problems.

    你好！我是中国科学技术大学数学专业研究生，导师为张磊教授。

    研究方向包括：语言模型形式化数学推理、具有持久记忆的智能体，以及大语言模型在安全领域的应用。
