About Me

I am a master's student in Mathematics at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), advised by Prof. Zhang Lei. My research background spans algebraic geometry, AI for mathematics (AI4Math), and AI memory systems. I received my bachelor's degree from the Gifted Young Programme at USTC in 2023.

My interest is in understanding and extending the boundaries of what AI systems can do. This includes making language models reason about and verify formal mathematical proofs, building agents that maintain coherent memory across long interactions, and applying AI to security analysis. I care about finding the edges of current AI capability and pushing them further.

I have worked on automated mathematical problem-solving and Lean 4 formalization at Beijing Zhongguancun AI Research Institute, on LLM fine-tuning for security alert classification at Chengting Technology, and on a multi-agent vulnerability discovery system at JD.com.
