About Me

I am a master's student in Mathematics at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), advised by Prof. Zhang Lei. My research is in computational mathematics and algebraic geometry. I received my bachelor's degree from the Gifted Young Programme at USTC in 2023.

I am interested in the intersection of AI and mathematics — specifically, whether large language models can assist in mathematical research, and what it means to formally verify a proof. I have worked on automated problem-solving pipelines and Lean 4 formalization during an internship at Beijing Zhongguancun AI Research Institute.

I have also interned at Chengting (Vulfocus) on LLM fine-tuning for security alert analysis, and at JD.com on a multi-agent vulnerability discovery system.
