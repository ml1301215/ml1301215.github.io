    Bogomolov Type Inequality on Quasi-Elliptic Surfaces 拟椭圆曲面上的 Bogomolov 不等式

    ← Publications

    Published in Journal of Mathematics, 2025 发表于《数学杂志》，2025年

    Download paper here 在此下载论文

    Recommended citation: Meng Lve. Bogomolov Type Inequality on Quasi-Elliptic Surfaces (拟椭圆曲面上的 Bogomolov 不等式)[J]. Journal of Mathematics, ISSN 0255-7797, 2025.

    Abstract

    Quasi-elliptic surfaces, discovered by Bombieri and Mumford in 1969, are a special class of algebraic surfaces that exist only in characteristics 2 and 3. Their distinctive geometric structure provides rich material for the study of algebraic geometry over fields of positive characteristic. These surfaces are the characteristic-\(p\) analogs of the \(\kappa = 1\) case in the Enriques–Kodaira classification, and their pathological properties make them key test cases for the scope of classical theorems.

    Geometrically, quasi-elliptic surfaces are characterized by their fibration structure: every quasi-elliptic surface \(S\) admits a quasi-elliptic fibration \(f \colon S \to C\), whose general fiber is a rational curve with arithmetic genus \(p_a = 1\) but geometric genus \(p_g = 0\), necessarily carrying an ordinary cusp singularity. This separation of arithmetic and geometric genus is a feature unique to fibrations in positive characteristic, absent from elliptic fibrations in characteristic zero.

    From an algebraic viewpoint, quasi-elliptic surfaces exhibit several pathological properties. First, they provide counterexamples to the Kodaira vanishing theorem: there exist ample line bundles \(\mathcal{L}\) with \(H^1(S,\mathcal{L}^{-1})\neq 0\). Second, they violate the classical Bogomolov inequality \(\Delta(\mathcal{E})\geq 0\). While the proof of Fujita's conjecture over \(\mathbb{C}\) relies on the Bogomolov inequality — making Fujita's conjecture appear to be a corollary of it — recent work of Yan-An Chen shows that Fujita's conjecture continues to hold on quasi-elliptic surfaces even though the Bogomolov inequality fails there. This phenomenon of partial survival suggests that positive-characteristic geometry may require a more refined theoretical framework.

    This paper studies the geometry of quasi-elliptic surfaces over algebraically closed fields of characteristic \(p > 0\) and the stability theory of vector bundles on them, focusing on a modified Bogomolov inequality for the positive-characteristic setting. In characteristic \(p = 3\), we prove: for any quasi-elliptic fibration \(f \colon S \to B\) and any \(L\)-semistable torsion-free sheaf \(E\), there exists a rational number \(a\) determined by the geometry of the fibration such that the modified inequality \[ \Delta(E) + \left(\frac{r(r-1)}{3}\right)^{\!2} \cdot c_0 \;\geq\; 0 \] holds, where the explicit expression for \(c_0\) splits into two cases according to \(b \geq 1\) and \(b = 0\).

    The proof proceeds in three steps: reduction to a stability problem on a ruled surface \(S_1\) via Frobenius base change of the fibration \(f \colon S \to B\); application of the Cartier descent theorem to connect the modified and classical inequalities; and computation of geometric invariants of the projective bundle \(\mathbb{P}(\mathcal{E})\) to determine the optimal correction term \(c_0\). This strategy may also provide methodological guidance for stability problems on other algebraic varieties in positive characteristic.

    Chapter 2 covers the theoretical background: the definition and basic properties of quasi-elliptic fibrations (singularity classification of general fibers, modified adjunction formula), and explicit constructions of Raynaud-type surfaces. Chapter 3 gives the detailed proof of the modified Bogomolov inequality, organized by the genus \(b = g(B)\) of the base curve, with explicit computation of the optimal correction term.

    拟椭圆曲面由Bombieri和Mumford于1969年发现，是一类仅存在于特征2和3域上的特殊代数曲面。其独特的几何结构为正特征域代数几何研究提供了丰富素材。这类曲面是Enriques–Kodaira分类中\(\kappa=1\)情形的特征\(p\)类比，其病态性质使其成为经典定理适用范围的关键检验案例。

    从几何角度看，拟椭圆曲面的特征在于其纤维化结构：每个拟椭圆曲面\(S\)均存在拟椭圆纤维化\(f\colon S\to C\)，其一般纤维为算术亏格\(p_a=1\)但几何亏格\(p_g=0\)的有理曲线，且必然带有普通尖点奇点。这种算术亏格与几何亏格的分离是正特征纤维化的独有特征，在特征零的椭圆纤维化中不存在。

    从代数角度看，拟椭圆曲面表现出若干病态性质。首先，它们提供了Kodaira消没定理的反例：存在丰富线丛\(\mathcal{L}\)使得\(H^1(S,\mathcal{L}^{-1})\neq 0\)。其次，它们违反了经典Bogomolov不等式\(\Delta(\mathcal{E})\geq 0\)。尽管复数域上Fujita猜想的证明依赖于Bogomolov不等式，但陈彦安的最新工作表明，即使Bogomolov不等式在拟椭圆曲面上不成立，Fujita猜想仍然成立。这种"部分幸存"现象表明，正特征几何可能需要更精细的理论框架。

    本文研究正特征代数闭域上拟椭圆曲面的几何及其上向量丛的稳定性理论，重点关注正特征情形下修正的Bogomolov不等式。在特征\(p=3\)时，我们证明：对任意拟椭圆纤维化\(f\colon S\to B\)及任意\(L\)-半稳定无挠层\(E\)，存在由纤维化几何确定的有理数\(a\)，使得修正不等式 \[ \Delta(E)+\left(\frac{r(r-1)}{3}\right)^{\!2}\cdot c_0\;\geq\;0 \] 成立，其中\(c_0\)的显式表达式按\(b\geq 1\)和\(b=0\)分两种情况给出。

    证明分三步：通过纤维化\(f\colon S\to B\)的Frobenius基变换，将问题归约为直纹曲面\(S_1\)上的稳定性问题；利用Cartier下降定理连接修正不等式与经典不等式；计算射影丛\(\mathbb{P}(\mathcal{E})\)的几何不变量以确定最优修正项\(c_0\)。
