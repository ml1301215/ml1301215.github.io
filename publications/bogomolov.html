Abstract

The Bogomolov inequality is a fundamental result in the theory of vector bundles on algebraic surfaces, bounding the discriminant of semistable bundles in terms of the first Chern class. Over fields of positive characteristic, quasi-elliptic surfaces — surfaces fibered over a curve with generic fiber a rational curve with a cusp — exhibit pathological behavior not present in characteristic zero, and the classical proof of the Bogomolov inequality breaks down. In this paper, we establish a Bogomolov type inequality for vector bundles on quasi-elliptic surfaces in characteristic two and three, using the Frobenius morphism and the theory of p-closed foliations. Our result extends the known range of surfaces for which a Bogomolov type bound holds in positive characteristic.
