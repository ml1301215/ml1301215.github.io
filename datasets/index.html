    Datasets

    This page lists all the datasets we have collected and used in our papers. To access them, please send an email to me (ml130@mail.ustc.edu.cn).

    MARD

    Problem sets and AI-generated solutions used in our work. Includes ICCM 2024/2025 competition problems, "First Proof" research-level problems, and model solutions generated by multiple LLMs. We manually labeled the AI-generated outputs and performed verification.

    Download github.com/ml1301215/question_sets-test_results

    Formalization Outcomes

    Lean 4 formalized proofs of ICCM competition problems.

    Download github.com/ml1301215/lean4-math-formalization

    TEEM Dataset

    Dataset associated with TEEM. Contains event sequences with temporal annotations for agent memory evaluation.

    Download github.com/ZXXZ1000/LATRACE
